## Products

The apps and services students and teachers use today.

### Applications

- **[Dyslexor](https://github.com/Dyslexor/Dyslexor)**
  The web app for students, teachers and heads of department: reading tools, training games, classes and announcements.
- **[Dyscalculia](https://github.com/Dyslexor/Dyscalculia)**
  *Dyslexor Maths*, the iOS and Android app for practising addition, subtraction, multiplication and division through quests and rewards.

### Services

Back-end services that power the web app.

- **[OCR](https://github.com/Dyslexor/OCR)**
  Turns scanned documents and photos into readable text.
- **[Spellchecker](https://github.com/Dyslexor/Spellchecker)**
  Spelling and writing checks, with simple explanations of each suggestion.

### Documentation

- **[DyslexorDocs](https://github.com/Dyslexor/DyslexorDocs)**
  Guides and reference material for the platform.
